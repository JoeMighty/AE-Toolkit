## 🎬 Highlighted Tool: Slideshow Builder Panel

The **Slideshow Builder** is a dockable UI script that instantly arranges selected layers into a sequential slideshow with custom durations, fades, and complex transitions. It intelligently handles both images and videos, saving hours of manual keyframing.

### ✨ Features
* **Dockable UI**: Integrates seamlessly seamlessly into your After Effects workspace.
* **Smart Media Filtering**: Choose to process Images Only, Videos Only, or a Mix of both.
* **Layer Ordering**: Arrange sequences from Top to Bottom or Bottom to Top.
* **Custom Durations**: Set specific global durations for images, videos, fades, and the final hold frame.
* **Dynamic Transitions**: Includes pre built animation transitions:
  * Fade
  * Fade + Scale
  * Fade + Slide Left / Right
  * Fade + Zoom In / Out
* **Auto Trimming**: Automatically trims video layers to your specified maximum duration and cuts the composition to the exact end of the final layer.
* **Keyframe Cleanup**: Optionally clears existing opacity and transform keys before building.

### 📦 Installation
1. Download the `SlideshowBuilder.jsx` file.
2. Place the script inside your After Effects `ScriptUI Panels` folder:
   * **Windows**: `C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects <version>\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\`
   * **Mac**: `/Applications/Adobe After Effects <version>/Scripts/ScriptUI Panels/`
3. Restart After Effects.
4. Open the tool by navigating to **Window > SlideshowBuilder** in the top menu.

### 🚀 Usage
1. Import your media into an After Effects composition.
2. Select the layers you want to include in the slideshow.
3. Open the **Slideshow Builder** panel.
4. Adjust your desired settings (durations, transition types, order).
5. Click **Build Slideshow**.
